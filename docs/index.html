The lesson

A deterministic feasibility study can be optimistic by a wide margin and still describe a project worth approving. This plant's approved NPV of €598.5 million sat roughly 3.5 times above the real Monte Carlo average of €172.9 million, yet the project cleared its acceptance thresholds in the large majority of simulated trials. The size of the gap between an approved number and its expected value changes the governance conversation, even when it doesn't change the decision.
